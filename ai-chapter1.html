Introduction to Artificial Intelligence

Learn the basics, history, applications and importance of Artificial Intelligence.

💡 Did You Know?

Artificial Intelligence (AI) is a branch of Computer Science that enables machines to perform tasks that normally require human intelligence.

📖 What is Artificial Intelligence?

Artificial Intelligence (AI) is the ability of machines or computer systems to think, learn, solve problems and make decisions like humans. AI helps computers perform intelligent tasks automatically.

⭐ Why do we use AI?

  • Automate repetitive tasks
  • Improve decision making
  • Reduce human effort
  • Increase accuracy
  • Solve complex problems

📚 History of AI

Year Event
1950 Alan Turing introduced the Turing Test.
1956 The term "Artificial Intelligence" was introduced by John McCarthy.
1997 IBM Deep Blue defeated World Chess Champion Garry Kasparov.
Today AI is widely used in healthcare, education, banking and transportation.
